We are hiring

Gardeners for the season ahead

Every season we welcome a small number of gardeners to our team. If you love working outdoors, take pride in careful, beautiful work, and would like to learn from a family company that has looked after Calgary gardens since 1995, we would be glad to hear from you.

Experience is welcome, but a love of plants matters more.

What we look for
I

A love of plants

Curiosity about how things grow, and the patience to do it right.

II

Care in the details

Clean edges, tidy beds, and a garden left better than you found it.

III

Reliability

Arriving when we said we would, every time.

IV

A good team member

Respect for our clients, their homes and each other.

How to apply

Send us a few words

Send your résumé and a short note about yourself to contact@perennialgardensyyc.ca. For more details about the role, please get in touch and we will be happy to tell you more.
